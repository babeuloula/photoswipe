Every day, PhotoSwipe shows you the photos and videos taken on this same day in previous years. A moment to relive your memories… and finally tidy them up.

✔ Swipe right to keep: the memory comes back next year.
🗑 Swipe left to mark it: nothing is deleted without your confirmation.
♥ Swipe up to add it to your favorites or share it.

When you are done, a summary shows the space you are about to free. One confirmation, and marked items go to your phone's trash, where you can restore them for about 30 days.

Missed a day? The last days stay on the home screen (a week by default, up to 30 days after time away), and an optional daily reminder lets you know when memories are left to sort.

Your photos stay with you: PhotoSwipe has no Internet access, needs no account and collects no data.

Made for everyone: buttons for every gesture, TalkBack, large fonts, reduced motion, light or dark theme.
